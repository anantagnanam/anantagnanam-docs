## Facts About the Sun

Every journey through the Solar System starts with its beating heart. Here are two facts that put our star in perspective.

<DidYouKnow variant="scale">
The Sun is so large that about **1.3 million Earths** could fit inside it — yet it is only a medium-sized star. Some stars are so enormous that millions of Suns would fit inside *them*.
</DidYouKnow>

<DidYouKnow variant="surprising">
Sunlight takes about **8 minutes and 20 seconds** to reach Earth. When you look at the Sun (never directly!), you are seeing it as it was over eight minutes ago.
</DidYouKnow>

## Facts About the Planets

Each of the eight planets keeps a few secrets. These are some of the best.

<DidYouKnow variant="surprising">
A day on Venus is longer than its year! Venus spins so slowly that one rotation takes 243 Earth days — while its orbit around the Sun takes only 225.
</DidYouKnow>

<DidYouKnow variant="scale">
Jupiter is so massive that it is more than **twice as heavy as all the other planets combined** — yet it spins faster than any planet, completing a day in under 10 hours.
</DidYouKnow>

<DidYouKnow variant="surprising">
Saturn would **float in water** if you found an ocean big enough. It is the least dense planet — lighter than water, ounce for ounce.
</DidYouKnow>

<DidYouKnow variant="scale">
Mars has the tallest volcano in the Solar System — **Olympus Mons** — roughly three times the height of Mount Everest, and so wide it would cover the whole of France.
</DidYouKnow>

## Facts About Moons and Beyond

The planets are only part of the story — their moons and the distant frontier have surprises of their own.

<DidYouKnow variant="surprising">
Our Moon drifts about **3.8 centimeters farther from Earth every year** — about the speed your fingernails grow. In the far future, total solar eclipses will no longer be possible.
</DidYouKnow>

<DidYouKnow variant="scale">
Jupiter's moon **Ganymede** is larger than the planet Mercury. And Europa, another of Jupiter's moons, hides a salty ocean beneath its icy shell.
</DidYouKnow>

<DidYouKnow variant="history">
The **Voyager 1** spacecraft, launched in 1977, is now over 24 billion kilometers from Earth — the farthest human-made object there is, carrying a golden record with sounds and pictures of our world.
</DidYouKnow>
