## Dwarf Planets — Small Worlds, Big Stories

In 2006, astronomers agreed on a new category for worlds that orbit the Sun but share their neighborhood with other objects: **dwarf planets**. Pluto, Eris, Makemake, Haumea, and Ceres belong to this club.

Pluto is not the only dwarf planet — and it is far from the edge. Billions of icy objects circle the Sun out here, and comets that visit the inner Solar System began their long journeys from regions like this.
</DidYouKnow>

## Where Does the Solar System End?

Even the Kuiper Belt is not the end. Beyond it lies a huge, spherical cloud of icy objects called the **Oort Cloud**, and eventually the point where the Sun's wind fades and interstellar space begins.

Only two human-made spacecraft, Voyager 1 and Voyager 2, have reached that edge so far — carrying golden records with sounds and pictures of Earth, messages in a bottle for any traveler who finds them.
