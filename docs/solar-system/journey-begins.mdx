## What Is the Solar System?

The **Solar System** is everything that travels around our Sun because of its gravity:

- The **Sun** — a star at the center
- **Eight planets** — Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune
- **Moons** — natural companions that circle planets
- **Dwarf planets**, asteroids, and comets
- Vast, empty space in between

Think of the Sun as the quiet heart of the neighborhood. It holds every planet in its place, the way a gentle thread holds beads on a necklace.

<DidYouKnow>
The word "solar" comes from "Sol", the Latin name for the Sun — so "Solar System" simply means "the Sun's family".
</DidYouKnow>
