## Just the Right Distance

Earth sits in what scientists sometimes call the **habitable zone** — not so close to the Sun that water boils away, and not so far that it freezes solid. This "Goldilocks" position lets water stay liquid, and liquid water is precious.

<DidYouKnow>
Earth is not perfectly round! It bulges slightly at the equator because it spins. And it is moving too — our whole planet orbits the Sun at about 107,000 km per hour.
</DidYouKnow>

## A Blanket of Air

Earth's atmosphere is a thin, life-giving blanket of gases — mostly nitrogen, with oxygen, water vapor, and a little carbon dioxide. It gives us air to breathe, burns up small space rocks before they reach us, and keeps our temperatures stable.

It is a very different blanket from Venus's. Ours lets heat escape in just the right way.

## Why Earth Matters

<WhyItMatters>
Earth is the only world we know that supports life. Understanding exactly why — the distance, the air, the water — helps us protect it, and teaches us what to look for when we search for other homes among the stars.
</WhyItMatters>
