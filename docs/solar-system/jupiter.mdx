## A Planet of Extremes

Jupiter is the **largest planet** in the Solar System — more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined. About 1,300 Earths could fit inside it.

Yet for all its size, it spins faster than any other planet — one day on Jupiter lasts barely 10 hours. That rapid spin stretches its clouds into those long, sweeping bands.

## A Family of Moons

Jupiter has at least 95 known moons. The four largest were spotted by Galileo in 1610 — the first time anyone had seen moons circling another world. That discovery changed how we think about our place in the universe.

<Callout type="tip" title="A moon worth remembering">
One of Jupiter's moons, Europa, is covered in a smooth shell of ice — with a hidden ocean of liquid water beneath it. Many scientists think it is one of the best places to search for life beyond Earth.
</Callout>

<DidYouKnow>
Jupiter acts like a giant shield for the inner planets. Its strong gravity catches or deflects many comets and asteroids that might otherwise head toward Earth.
</DidYouKnow>
